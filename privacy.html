STBPlay Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 (version 0.9.16)

App: STBPlay for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Publisher: LoopX Studio

Privacy and support contact: krsptrbeez@gmail.com

1. What STBPlay does

STBPlay is a media player for playlists and provider accounts that you supply. On first use the app asks you to confirm that you have the right to use the sources you add; it does not download, convert or save media. It does not provide channels, movies, subscriptions or an STBPlay account. The publisher does not operate a server that receives your profiles, credentials or viewing history. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK or automatic crash-reporting service.

2. Information you enter and requests to your provider

Depending on the source you choose, you may enter a server/playlist URL, provider username and password, or a provider-registered MAC identifier. The MAC is manually entered; STBPlay does not read your device's hardware identifiers or advertising identifier. These details may be transmitted to your chosen provider to authenticate and obtain its catalog and streams. Playlist and stream URLs may themselves contain credentials or tokens.

Provider and content/CDN servers also receive the requests needed to browse categories, search supported catalogs, select episodes, load streams and retrieve media. Such requests can reveal the title or channel requested, searches sent to the provider, your IP address, session cookies and ordinary technical headers, including the emulated STB model used by Stalker portals. If a provider refuses the app's default request, STBPlay may retry using a generic player name (for example FFmpeg or VLC) as its user agent; this only changes what your provider sees. STBPlay does not sell this information or send it to a developer analytics service. External providers are independent; their own privacy policies, access rules and retention practices apply. The publisher cannot access or delete their records.

3. Information stored on your device

The app keeps saved source profiles, the last-used source details, cached catalog metadata, favorites, recent viewing, playback positions and player preferences in the app's private storage. A saved profile is created only when you enter a Profile Name and connection details and press Save in Sources; connecting is done from the saved playlist list with Connect / Refresh, and does not create one by itself, but the app can retain last-used connection details for convenience. Sensitive profile fields, cached playback addresses and commands, favorites and recent-viewing payloads are encrypted locally with AES-GCM using a data key protected by the Apple Keychain. Catalog titles, groups, record identifiers and some settings/progress metadata are not a fully encrypted database. Local protection does not guarantee security on a jailbroken or compromised device.

On Apple TV the system may remove cached files when storage runs low, so saved source profiles are also kept in the Keychain.

Optional app-opening PIN protection stores a salted PIN verifier rather than the PIN itself. It is an app lock, not an age-rating or content-filtering system. While the PIN lock is on, the app hides its contents in the app switcher. Session cookies are held in memory during use. The app's data files are marked to be excluded from iCloud and device backups; Keychain items are kept on this device only.

4. Permissions and device information

STBPlay uses network access for connections and playback, and, on iPhone and iPad, may ask for local network access when a source is on your home network. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising-identifier tracking or photo-library permissions. It does not infer or store your location. Network servers can nevertheless see an IP address and may process it under their own policies. If you choose to import subtitles on iPhone or iPad, the system file picker grants access to the selected file; a copy is kept in the app's private storage so it can be reused. STBPlay does not upload subtitle files to the publisher. TV guide: when your source provides a program guide, STBPlay downloads it directly from your provider (for example the guide address in your playlist, the Xtream guide file, or the portal's short program list) while you use Live TV, and stores the program titles and times locally on your device. Guide data is not sent to the publisher. The trial and pass notices are shown inside the app; STBPlay does not request notification permission.

STBPlay Pass: after a 30-day free trial, playback requires an optional pass bought through the App Store (1 month or 1 year, one payment, no automatic renewal). Apple processes the payment under its own terms; STBPlay never receives or stores your card or payment details, and the publisher operates no payment server. So that the trial does not restart after a reinstall, STBPlay saves the trial start date in the Keychain and in the app's settings on your device. This date is not sent to the publisher.

5. HTTP and HTTPS

STBPlay supports HTTP and HTTPS sources. HTTPS encrypts the connection when supported by every endpoint involved. HTTP does not encrypt credentials, identifiers or viewing requests in transit. Local encryption does not make HTTP traffic encrypted. Use a provider with HTTPS support where available. The app does not disable TLS certificate validation. Apple's system player follows redirects itself, so an HTTPS stream address that a provider redirects to plain HTTP may be followed.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until removed through the app or by deleting the app. Deleting a saved profile removes its associated cached catalog, saved favorites and source history, and forgets matching last-used source credentials. Settings offers Clear watch history and Clear all local data. Clearing all local data removes profiles, credentials, favorites, viewing history, preferences and the optional PIN; the current PIN is requested when the app lock is enabled. The free-trial start date is kept so that clearing data cannot restart the trial. This does not cancel or delete an external provider account. Contact that provider about its account, subscription or server-held data. There is no publisher-operated STBPlay account to delete.

7. Support messages

If you email the support address, you voluntarily provide your email address and anything included in the message. The publisher uses these messages to answer support/privacy requests and retains them as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. Email is handled by the publisher's email provider under its policies. Do not send provider passwords, MAC identifiers, private playlist URLs or unredacted logs. You may email the privacy contact to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. STBPlay itself does not automatically send support messages or logs.

8. Children and external content

STBPlay is not specifically directed to children. It supplies no content and cannot guarantee the suitability of external catalogs. Parents should choose appropriate sources and control provider accounts. An optional PIN does not replace supervision or a provider's parental controls.

9. Changes and contact

The publisher will update this policy and its App Store privacy disclosures if the app's behavior changes. The effective date identifies this revision. For privacy or support requests, contact krsptrbeez@gmail.com and identify STBPlay. The publisher does not have a remote copy of your local profiles or history to retrieve.
