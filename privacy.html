STBPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: LoopX Studio · Contact: support.stbplay@loopxstudio.ca

This policy covers the STBPlay apps published by LoopX Studio on every platform where STBPlay is available. The apps behave the same way on the points below. Differences between platforms are listed in section 10, “Platform notes”.

1. What STBPlay does

STBPlay is a media player for playlists and provider accounts that you supply. On first use the app asks you to confirm that you have the right to use the sources you add; it does not download, convert or save media. It does not provide channels, movies, subscriptions or an STBPlay account. The publisher does not operate a server that receives your profiles, credentials or viewing history. The apps contain no advertising SDK, analytics SDK or automatic crash-reporting service.

2. Information you enter and requests to your provider

Depending on the source you choose, you may enter a server or playlist URL, a provider username and password, or a provider-registered MAC identifier. The MAC identifier is entered by you; STBPlay does not read your device’s hardware identifiers or advertising identifier. These details may be transmitted to your chosen provider to authenticate and obtain its catalog and streams. Playlist and stream URLs may themselves contain credentials or tokens.

Provider and content/CDN servers also receive the requests needed to browse categories, search supported catalogs, select episodes, load streams and retrieve media. Such requests can reveal the title or channel requested, searches sent to the provider, your IP address, session cookies and ordinary technical headers, including the emulated set-top-box model used by Stalker-style portals. If a provider refuses the app’s default request, STBPlay may retry using a generic player name (for example FFmpeg or VLC) as its user agent; this only changes what your provider sees. STBPlay does not sell this information or send it to a developer analytics service. External providers are independent; their own privacy policies, access rules and retention practices apply. The publisher cannot access or delete their records.

3. Information stored on your device

The app keeps saved source profiles, the last-used source details, cached catalog metadata, favorites, recent viewing, playback positions and player preferences in the app’s private storage on your device. A saved profile is created only when you enter a Profile Name and connection details and press Save in Sources; connecting from the saved playlist list does not create one by itself, but the app can retain last-used connection details for convenience.

Sensitive profile fields, cached playback addresses and commands, favorites and recent-viewing payloads are encrypted locally with AES-GCM, using a key protected by your platform’s secure key storage. Catalog titles, groups, record identifiers and some settings and progress metadata are not a fully encrypted database. Local protection does not guarantee security on a rooted, jailbroken or otherwise compromised device.

The optional PIN lock stores a salted PIN verifier rather than the PIN itself. It is an app lock and an adult-label filter that matches category names; it is not an age-rating or full content-filtering system. Session cookies are held in memory during use. App data is excluded from device and cloud backups where the platform allows it.

4. Permissions and device information

STBPlay uses network access for connections and playback. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising-identifier tracking or broad photo or media-library permissions, and it does not infer or store your location. Network servers can nevertheless see an IP address and may process it under their own policies.

If you choose to import subtitles, the system file picker grants access to the selected file only; STBPlay does not upload subtitle files to the publisher.

TV guide. When your source provides a program guide, STBPlay downloads it directly from your provider while you use Live TV and stores the program titles and times locally on your device. Guide data is not sent to the publisher.

Trial and pass notices are shown inside the app; STBPlay does not request notification permission.

5. Free trial and passes

STBPlay includes a 30-day free trial. After that, playback requires an optional pass (1 month or 1 year, one payment, no automatic renewal) bought through the store of your device. The store processes the payment under its own terms; STBPlay never receives or stores your card or payment details, and the publisher operates no payment server. So that the trial does not restart after a reinstall, STBPlay saves the trial start date on your device, in private app storage and in secure platform storage where available. This date is not sent to the publisher.

6. HTTP and HTTPS

STBPlay supports HTTP and HTTPS sources. HTTPS encrypts the connection when supported by every endpoint involved. HTTP does not encrypt credentials, identifiers or viewing requests in transit, and local encryption does not change that. Use a provider with HTTPS support where available. The app does not disable TLS certificate validation.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you remove it through the app, through your device’s app-storage settings, or by uninstalling the app. Deleting a saved profile removes its associated cached catalog, saved favorites and source history, and forgets matching last-used source credentials. Settings offers Clear watch history and Clear all local data. Clearing all local data removes profiles, credentials, favorites, viewing history, preferences and the optional PIN; the current PIN is requested when the app lock is enabled. The free-trial start date is kept so that clearing data cannot restart the trial. This does not cancel or delete an external provider account: contact that provider about its account, subscription or server-held data. There is no publisher-operated STBPlay account to delete.

8. Support messages

If you email the support address, you voluntarily provide your email address and anything included in the message. The publisher uses these messages to answer support and privacy requests and keeps them as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Email is handled by the publisher’s email provider under its policies. Do not send provider passwords, MAC identifiers, private playlist URLs or unredacted logs. You may email the privacy contact to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. STBPlay itself does not automatically send support messages or logs.

9. This website

This website (stbplay.loopxstudio.ca) contains no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, forms or third-party fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives website requests and logs visitor IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement; the domain’s DNS is provided by Cloudflare. The home page links to store listings, which are run by those stores under their own policies. Website hosting is separate from the app’s provider connections.

10. Platform notes

Apple (iPhone, iPad, Apple TV)

  • Sensitive data is encrypted with a key protected by the Apple Keychain. On Apple TV the system may remove cached files when storage runs low, so saved source profiles are also kept in the Keychain.
  • On iPhone and iPad the app may ask for local network access when a source is on your home network. Imported subtitle files are copied into the app’s private storage so they can be reused.
  • While the PIN lock is on, the app hides its contents in the app switcher. The app’s data files are marked to be excluded from iCloud and device backups; Keychain items stay on the device.
  • Passes are bought through the App Store and Apple processes the payment. The trial start date is saved in the Keychain and in the app’s settings.
  • Apple’s system player follows redirects itself, so an HTTPS stream address that a provider redirects to plain HTTP may be followed.
  • Apple’s App Privacy label for STBPlay is “Data Not Collected”.

Android and Fire OS

  • Sensitive data is encrypted with a key protected by Android Keystore. The app requests only Internet and network-state access and does not request notification permission. Imported subtitles use Android’s document picker.
  • App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and app-controlled device transfer; device or vendor behavior outside the app’s control may differ. Clearing all local data closes the app.
  • Passes are bought through the store of your device (Google Play on Android devices, the Amazon Appstore on Fire OS devices). The trial start date is saved in private app storage and, where available, in Android Block Store.
  • The app does not silently follow an HTTPS-to-HTTP redirect.

Other platforms

When STBPlay becomes available on another platform, this page will be updated before release to describe any differences.

11. Children and external content

STBPlay is not specifically directed to children. It supplies no content and cannot guarantee the suitability of external catalogs. Parents should choose appropriate sources and control provider accounts. An optional PIN does not replace supervision or a provider’s parental controls.

12. Changes and contact

The publisher will update this policy and its store privacy disclosures if the apps’ behavior changes. The date at the top identifies this revision. For privacy or support requests, contact support.stbplay@loopxstudio.ca and mention STBPlay. The publisher does not have a remote copy of your local profiles or history to retrieve.
