STBPlay Support

Help for STBPlay on every platform.

STBPlay is a media player. You add your own playlists and provider accounts, and STBPlay plays them. It does not include channels, movies or playlists of its own.

Contact

Email support.stbplay@loopxstudio.ca and put “STBPlay” in the subject. Please tell us:

  • your platform and device model (for example Fire OS, Android, iPhone, Apple TV);
  • the app version (Settings > About);
  • what you were doing and what happened.

Please do not send provider passwords, MAC identifiers or private playlist URLs.

Common questions

The app has no channels. STBPlay does not provide content. Add your own M3U / M3U8 playlist, Xtream-style account or Stalker-style portal under Sources.

Passes and trial. STBPlay includes a 30-day free trial. Afterwards you can buy a 1 Month or 1 Year pass (one payment, no automatic renewal) through the store on your device. If you reinstall the app or change device, use the restore option on the pass screen where your store provides one.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by the store where you bought the pass, so refund requests go to that store. For Apple purchases, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Playback or login problems. Quality and availability depend on your provider and your network. If your provider reports that your subscription has expired or is blocked, contact that provider.
