STBPlay Support

STBPlay is a media player for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. It plays playlists and provider accounts that you supply. It does not include channels, movies or playlists.

Contact

Email support.stbplay@loopxstudio.ca and include "STBPlay" in the subject, your device model and the app version (Settings > About). Please do not send provider passwords, MAC identifiers or private playlist URLs.

Common questions

The app has no channels. STBPlay does not provide content. Add your own M3U/M3U8, Xtream or Stalker source under Sources.

Passes and trial. STBPlay includes a 30-day free trial. Afterwards you can buy a 1 Month or 1 Year pass (one payment, no automatic renewal) through the App Store. Use Restore Purchases on the pass screen if you reinstall or change device.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple; request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy. See the Privacy Policy.
